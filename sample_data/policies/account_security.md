# VaaniDesk Account Security Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Audience:** All registered customers

## Account Creation & Verification

Registration requires valid mobile number (OTP) and email verification within 72 hours. Unverified accounts cannot place COD orders above ₹2,000 or access wallet withdrawals.

## Password Requirements

- Minimum 10 characters
- At least one uppercase, lowercase, digit, and symbol
- Cannot match last 5 passwords
- Bcrypt-hashed storage; VaaniDesk staff **never** ask for your password

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Optional TOTP authenticator or SMS OTP at login. **Required** for:

- Wallet withdrawals > ₹1,000
- Address book changes + order placement within same session
- Gift card redemption > ₹5,000

Enable MFA under **Settings → Security → Two-Step Verification**.

## Session Management

- Web sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity (7 days without "Remember device")
- Mobile app: biometric unlock recommended; PIN fallback allowed
- View active sessions and **Sign out all devices** in Security settings

## Suspicious Activity Response

We may temporarily lock accounts when:

- Multiple failed login attempts from new geolocation
- Rapid high-value orders on dormant accounts
- Payment instrument mismatch patterns

Unlock via email link + OTP or support identity verification (last order ID + billing PIN fragment).

## Phishing & Impersonation

Official VaaniDesk domains: `vaanidesk.in`, `app.vaanidesk.in`, `support.vaanidesk.in`. Report suspicious messages to **security@vaanidesk.in**. We do not contact you on WhatsApp for password resets unless you initiated chat from verified business account.

## Data Breach Notification

If a breach affects credentials, we notify within **72 hours** per applicable law and force password reset on next login.

## Account Deletion

**Settings → Privacy → Delete Account** initiates 14-day grace period. Pending orders must complete or cancel first. Order history anonymized after grace; tax records retained 7 years per regulation.
