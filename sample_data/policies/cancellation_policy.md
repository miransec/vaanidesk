# VaaniDesk Order Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines when and how customers may cancel orders placed on VaaniDesk platforms, including pre-shipment cancellations, post-shipment intercepts, and subscription or pre-order exceptions.

## Cancellation Windows by Order State

### Pending Payment / Payment Failed

Orders in **Pending Payment** auto-expire after 30 minutes. No action required. If payment failed but you wish to retry, use **Complete Payment** within 24 hours—do not place a duplicate order.

### Confirmed, Not Yet Shipped

You may self-cancel from **My Orders** without reason until the status changes to **Processing at Warehouse**. Typical window: 2–8 hours after payment confirmation during business hours.

After **Processing at Warehouse**, cancellation is no longer guaranteed. Submit a cancellation request via chat; if pick has not started, we cancel and refund within 3–5 business days.

### Shipped / Out for Delivery

Self-service cancellation is disabled. Options:

1. **Refuse delivery** (COD or prepaid)—parcel RTOs and refund follows return policy timeline.
2. **Accept and return** under return procedure within eligible return window.

### Customized / Made-to-Order SKUs

Engraved, configured, or perishable custom items are **non-cancellable** once production starts (status: **In Production**). Preview approval emails constitute acceptance of non-cancellable terms.

## Cancellation by VaaniDesk

We may cancel orders without penalty to you when:

- Product discontinued or stock discrepancy discovered
- Pricing error (>15% deviation from intended price)
- Fraud or policy violation suspected
- Force majeure affecting fulfillment region

You receive full refund plus optional ₹100 goodwill coupon for involuntary cancellations (except fraud cases).

## Refund Method & Timeline After Cancellation

| Payment method | Refund destination | Timeline |
|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| UPI / Cards / Net banking | Original instrument | 3–5 business days |
| Wallets | VaaniDesk wallet or source wallet | 1–3 business days |
| COD (if prepaid component) | Bank transfer or wallet | 5–7 business days |
| EMI | Lender reversal per bank SLA | 7–14 business days |

## Partial Cancellations

Multi-item orders: cancel individual line items before shipment if each SKU ships independently. Bundle discounts may recalculate; price difference settled via refund or small balance charge with email confirmation.

## Subscription Cancellations

VaaniDesk Subscribe & Save: cancel anytime before next billing date. Already-shipped cycles are not refundable; skip-next-delivery available once per quarter.

## How to Cancel

1. App / web: **My Orders → Select order → Cancel**
2. Email: **cancel@vaanidesk.in** with order ID
3. Phone IVR: Press 2 after order verification OTP
