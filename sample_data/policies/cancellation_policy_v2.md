# VaaniDesk Order Cancellation Policy (v2)

**Effective date:** 1 April 2026

## Key Changes from v1

- Extended self-cancel window during **Processing at Warehouse** if pick scan not recorded
- Introduced **Instant Cancel** for select prepaid metro orders (fee-free)
- Clarified marketplace seller cancellation SLAs

## Instant Cancel (New)

Eligible orders display **Instant Cancel** until carrier manifest upload. Criteria:

- Prepaid only
- Origin FC in Bangalore, Mumbai, or Delhi NCR
- No customized line items
- Order value under ₹50,000

Refund initiates within **15 minutes** to original payment method (subject to bank posting).

## Warehouse Processing Grace Period

If status is **Processing at Warehouse** but no **Pick Completed** scan exists for 4+ hours, self-cancel reopens automatically. This addresses overnight queue backlog.

## Marketplace (Third-Party Seller) Orders

Seller-managed inventory: cancellation requests route to seller dashboard. Seller must respond within **24 hours**. If seller ships despite timely cancel request, VaaniDesk escalates and guarantees refund upon RTO or return.

## Subscription v2

Subscribe & Save now supports **pause up to 90 days** (was skip-only). Pause does not cancel promotional pricing lock if resumed within 90 days.

## Refund Timelines (Unchanged from v1)

See refund_timeline.md for consolidated matrix.
