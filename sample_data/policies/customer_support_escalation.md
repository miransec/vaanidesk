# VaaniDesk Customer Support Escalation Guide

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Audience:** Customers and frontline support agents

## Support Tiers

| Tier | Channel | Hours (IST) | Typical scope |
|------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| L1 | Chatbot + FAQ | 24/7 | Tracking, password reset, policy links |
| L2 | Live chat / email | 8:00–22:00 | Returns, refunds, damage claims |
| L3 | Specialist queue | 10:00–18:00 Mon–Sat | Payment disputes, legal, repeat failures |
| L4 | Leadership review | By appointment | Regulatory complaints, media escalations |

## When to Escalate (Customer Self-Service)

Escalate when:

- L2 ticket open **>5 business days** without resolution
- Promised callback missed twice
- Conflicting information from two agents
- Financial impact **> ₹10,000** unresolved
- Safety or legal concern (injury, fire hazard product)

## Escalation Methods

1. In ticket: **Request Escalation** button (appears after 48 hours on open L2 tickets)
2. Email: **escalations@vaanidesk.in** with ticket ID in subject `[ESC-XXXXX]`
3. Phone: IVR option 9 after OTP verification (queue position announced)

## SLA Commitments

| Escalation level | First human response | Target resolution |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Standard escalation | 24 hours | 5 business days |
| Priority (Plus members) | 12 hours | 3 business days |
| Safety-critical | 4 hours | 48 hours |

## Information to Include

- Order ID(s) and registered mobile
- Prior ticket IDs and agent names if known
- Timeline of events (dates, amounts)
- Attachments already submitted (avoid duplicates)

## Ombudsman & External Routes

If internal escalation fails after **15 business days**, request **Final Response Letter** for consumer forum / mediation. VaaniDesk participates in FICCI e-commerce mediation pilot for eligible disputes under ₹5 lakh.

## Agent Notes (Internal Summary for RAG)

Agents should never promise outcomes outside published policy. Use phrase: "I'll align with our published policy and confirm in writing."
