# VaaniDesk Customer Support Escalation — Agent Playbook Supplement

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Audience:** L2/L3 agents (authenticated access)

## De-Escalation Scripts (Approved Language)

Use empathetic acknowledgment without admitting liability beyond policy:

- "I understand this delay is frustrating, especially given the amount involved."
- "Let me review the timeline against our published refund SLA and confirm next steps in writing."
- "I don't have authority to bypass QC, but I can escalate to a specialist who can review your photos again."

Avoid: "It's your fault," "Policy is policy," "There's nothing I can do."

## Repeat Contact Handling

If customer contacts **3+ times** in 48 hours on same issue:

1. Link tickets in CRM
2. Flag **Repeat Contact** for L3 queue
3. Offer single point of contact email alias when available

## Compensation Authority Matrix (Agents)

| Scenario | L2 max | L3 max |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Shipping delay > 5 days past SLA | ₹100 wallet | ₹300 wallet |
| Wrong item (keep vs return) | Return label only | + ₹150 goodwill |
| Rude prior agent (verified) | Apology email | ₹100 wallet |

Amounts above matrix require manager approval ticket.

## Regulatory Keywords

Messages containing "consumer court," "lawyer," "FIR," "newspaper," or "social media viral" auto-route to L3 with **Regulatory Sensitivity** tag. Response SLA: 12 hours.
