# VaaniDesk Damaged Products Policy (v2)

**Effective date:** 20 February 2026

## Scope

This policy covers products that arrive **physically damaged**, **defective on arrival (DOA)**, or **tampered in transit**. Cosmetic damage to outer packaging alone—without product damage—is handled under separate packaging feedback, not this policy.

## Refund & Replacement for Damaged Products

Customers who report a **damaged product**, **defective product**, or **damaged-on-arrival** claim within the reporting window may receive:

| Severity | Typical resolution |
|----------|-------------------|
| Minor cosmetic (product functional) | Partial **refund** 5–15% or accessory coupon |
| Functional damage / missing parts | Free replacement part or full unit **replacement** |
| Total loss / irreparable | **Full refund** + return pickup if unit received |
| Safety hazard (exposed wires, leaking battery) | Immediate **refund**; do not use product |

This is the authoritative customer refund/return guidance for damaged items.

## Reporting Timeline

You must report damage within:

- **Perishable / food:** 24 hours of delivery timestamp
- **Electronics & appliances:** 72 hours
- **Furniture & fragile décor:** 48 hours
- **General merchandise:** 7 days

Late reports may be declined unless you provide carrier POD photos showing pre-existing transit damage.

## Required Evidence

Submit via **My Orders → Report Issue → Damaged on Arrival**:

1. Clear photos of shipping label and outer box (all sides if possible)
2. Photos of inner packaging and damaged product
3. Short video (optional but recommended for electronics) showing power-on failure or visible crack
4. IMEI / serial number photo for serialized goods

Do not discard packaging until claim is closed—carrier audits may require inspection.

## What's New in v2

- **AI-assisted triage** for photo uploads (instant preliminary approval for clear cases)
- **Advance replacement** for VaaniDesk Plus members on electronics ≥ ₹5,000
- Expanded **leaking / hazardous liquid** protocol

## AI Photo Triage

When you upload evidence, our system classifies damage severity within **2 minutes** for 80% of cases. Unclear photos receive a checklist resubmit request—avoid blurry or dark images.

## VaaniDesk Plus Advance Replacement

Members with active Plus subscription may receive replacement shipment **before** pickup of damaged unit when:

- Claim filed within 48 hours
- Stock available at nearest FC
- Hold placed on payment method equal to replacement value (released when damaged unit scanned at hub)

## Hazardous Products Protocol

For leaking batteries, aerosols, or broken glass:

1. Do not ship back without approved pickup
2. Keep away from heat sources
3. Support may authorize **dispose locally + full refund** with photo proof if pickup SLA exceeds 72 hours

## Marketplace SLA

Third-party sellers must acknowledge damage tickets within **12 hours** (down from 24). VaaniDesk auto-refunds if seller misses SLA and customer evidence meets confidence threshold.

## Exclusions

Not covered under damaged products policy:

- Damage from customer mishandling after delivery
- Wear from attempted DIY repair
- Products missing warranty seal because customer opened for inspection beyond visual check (unless DOA proven)
- Damage reported after return window without transit proof
