# VaaniDesk Delivery Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Applies to:** All VaaniDesk retail channels (web, mobile app, partner kiosks)

## Overview

VaaniDesk ships physical goods across India through contracted logistics partners. This policy explains delivery timelines, shipping charges, tracking, failed deliveries, and regional exceptions. Delivery commitments apply from the moment your order is marked **Shipped** in your account—not from the payment confirmation timestamp.

## Standard Delivery Timelines

| Zone | Metro cities | Tier-2 cities | Rural / remote PIN codes |
|------|--------------|---------------|---------------------------|
| Standard | 3–5 business days | 5–7 business days | 7–12 business days |
| Express | 1–2 business days | 2–4 business days | Not available |
| Same-day (select SKUs) | Order before 11:00 IST | Not available | Not available |

Business days exclude Sundays and national public holidays observed by our primary carrier (Delhivery / Blue Dart rotation). Orders placed after 18:00 IST are processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Charges

- Orders above ₹999: **Free standard shipping** on eligible SKUs (see product page badge).
- Orders below ₹999: Flat ₹79 standard / ₹149 express.
- Bulky items (furniture, large appliances): Calculated at checkout; includes stair carry surcharge where applicable.
- Cash-on-delivery (COD): Additional ₹49 handling fee; max COD order value ₹25,000.

## Order Tracking

Once shipped, you receive SMS and email with a tracking link. Tracking updates may lag 6–12 hours after the first scan at the origin hub. If tracking shows no movement for **72 hours**, open a support ticket with order ID—do not assume loss until we confirm with the carrier.

## Failed Delivery & Reattempts

Carriers attempt delivery up to **three times** over five business days. After the third failed attempt:

1. The parcel returns to our regional fulfillment center.
2. We notify you by email within 24 hours of RTO (return-to-origin) receipt.
3. You may request reshipment (shipping charges may apply) or a full refund within 7 days of RTO notification.

Common failure reasons: incorrect address, customer unavailable, COD refusal, or security gate restrictions. Always provide an alternate contact number at checkout.

## Address Changes After Shipment

Address changes are **not guaranteed** once a label is printed. Contact support within **2 hours** of shipment notification for a best-effort intercept. VaaniDesk is not liable for misdelivery caused by customer-provided incorrect PIN codes or incomplete addresses.

## Restricted & Remote Areas

Certain PIN codes are temporarily unserviceable due to weather, local restrictions, or carrier coverage gaps. If checkout blocks your PIN, register for back-in-stock / delivery alerts—we refresh serviceability weekly.

## Delivery Proof & Signature

High-value orders (≥ ₹15,000) require OTP verification or signature at delivery. Photo proof of delivery (POD) is retained by carriers for 90 days and can be shared on dispute requests.
