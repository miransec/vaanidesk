# VaaniDesk Delivery Policy (v2 — Updated March 2026)

**Effective date:** 15 March 2026  
**Supersedes:** delivery_policy.md (January 2026 edition)  
**Applies to:** All VaaniDesk retail channels

## Overview

This revision introduces **GreenRoute** consolidated shipping for multi-item orders, extends express coverage to 40 additional tier-2 PIN clusters, and clarifies festival-season SLA adjustments.

## Standard Delivery Timelines (Revised)

| Zone | Metro cities | Tier-2 cities | Rural / remote PIN codes |
|------|--------------|---------------|---------------------------|
| Standard | 2–4 business days | 4–6 business days | 6–10 business days |
| Express | 1 business day (order by 14:00 IST) | 2–3 business days | 5–7 business days (new) |
| Same-day | Order before 10:00 IST | Pilot in Pune, Jaipur | Not available |

During Diwali, Holi, and year-end peak (15 Nov–5 Jan), add **+2 business days** to standard and express estimates shown at checkout.

## Shipping Charges (Updated)

- Free standard shipping threshold lowered to **₹799** on eligible SKUs.
- Sub-₹799 orders: ₹69 standard / ₹129 express.
- **GreenRoute bundle discount:** Additional 10% off shipping when 3+ small-parcel items ship together from the same FC.
- COD handling remains ₹49; max COD value increased to **₹30,000** for verified accounts (KYC tier 1).

## Carbon-Neutral Option

At checkout, opt into **GreenRoute+** (₹19) to offset last-mile emissions via verified REC credits. Receipt includes certificate ID in order history.

## Tracking & Proactive Alerts

We now push in-app notifications at: label created, out for delivery, and delivery attempt failed. WhatsApp tracking opt-in available in notification settings.

## Failed Delivery (Updated)

Four attempts over **seven** business days before RTO (previously three over five). After RTO, auto-refund triggers within **48 hours** unless you select reship in the RTO email link.

## Address Changes

Intercept window extended to **4 hours** post-shipment for metro hubs only. Tier-2 and rural intercepts remain best-effort within 2 hours.

## Restricted Areas

Serviceability API refreshes **daily** (previously weekly). Temporary embargoes display estimated resume date on the product page.

## Contact

**delivery@vaanidesk.in** | WhatsApp bot keyword: `TRACK <order_id>`
