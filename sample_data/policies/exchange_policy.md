# VaaniDesk Exchange Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## Overview

Exchanges replace return-plus-reorder for size, color, and variant swaps when stock exists. One exchange per line item unless first exchange shipment was VaaniDesk error.

## Eligible Categories

- Apparel & footwear (unworn, tags intact)
- Fashion accessories
- Select home textiles (unopened packaging)
- Electronics **only** when wrong variant shipped by VaaniDesk

Non-eligible: personalized goods, hygiene, opened software, clearance **Final Sale** SKUs.

## Exchange Window

**10 days** from delivery (same as standard return). Plus members: **15 days** on fashion (see return_procedure_v2.md).

## Exchange Process

1. **My Orders → Exchange → Choose replacement variant**
2. System reserves replacement stock **24 hours** pending pickup schedule
3. Courier delivers replacement and collects original (single visit when possible)
4. If replacement unavailable: convert to return refund or waitlist notification

## Price Difference Handling

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Exchange higher price | Pay difference at confirmation (UPI/card) |
| Exchange lower price | Refund difference within 3 business days |
| Same price | No adjustment |

## Condition Standards

Same QC rules as returns. Worn or washed apparel exchanges rejected; original item returned to customer at their shipping cost if unreachable.

## Multi-Attempt Exchanges

Second exchange attempt on same order line requires L2 approval (e.g., repeated size chart issues).

## Marketplace Exchanges

Seller inventory: seller must confirm variant within **24 hours** or VaaniDesk offers refund alternative.
