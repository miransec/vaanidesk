# VaaniDesk Privacy of Support Chats Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## Scope

This policy describes how VaaniDesk collects, stores, uses, and protects data from **live chat**, **async messaging**, **WhatsApp Business** threads, **email support**, and **IVR transcripts** when linked to your account.

## Data We Collect in Support Interactions

- Message content and attachments you send
- Metadata: timestamps, agent ID, channel, device type, app version
- Order IDs and phone numbers you provide for verification
- Bot session intent labels (e.g., "return_status") for routing

We do **not** request full card numbers or CVV in chat. Agents use masked payment references only.

## Purpose of Processing

- Resolve your request and train quality assurance reviewers
- Improve chatbot intent models using **redacted** historical transcripts
- Detect fraud patterns (multi-account abuse, social engineering)
- Comply with legal holds and regulatory requests

## Retention Periods

| Channel | Active storage | Archive | Deletion |
|---------|---------------|---------|----------|
| In-app chat | 18 months | 7 years anonymized | On account deletion request after legal hold clear |
| Email | 24 months | 7 years | Same |
| WhatsApp | 12 months | 5 years | Same |
| IVR audio | 90 days | 1 year transcript | Auto-delete |

Marketing opt-out does not delete support history required for transaction audit.

## Human Review & AI Assistance

VaaniDesk uses AI **copilot** suggestions for agents. Copilot does not auto-send messages without human approval except L1 bot within published FAQ boundaries.

Your chats may be sampled for QA scoring (5% random sample monthly). Sensitive categories (health-related product questions) flagged for restricted QA team.

## Third-Party Processors

Chat infrastructure hosted on ISO 27001-certified cloud in **India region** primary with DR in secondary Indian AZ. Subprocessors listed at **vaanidesk.in/subprocessors**. WhatsApp messages subject to Meta's terms in addition to this policy.

## Your Rights

- **Access:** Export last 12 months of support transcripts via Privacy Center
- **Correction:** Factual corrections to order-linked notes (not agent subjective assessments)
- **Deletion:** Request deletion where no legal retention obligation; may retain minimal audit stub

Submit requests: **privacy@vaanidesk.in** | Response within **30 days**

## Security Controls

TLS 1.3 in transit; AES-256 at rest; role-based access for agents; session timeout 15 minutes on agent workstations; DLP blocks outbound paste of Aadhaar/PAN full numbers.

## Breach Notification

Material breach affecting chat content triggers notification per DPDP Act timelines and this policy's account security cross-reference.
