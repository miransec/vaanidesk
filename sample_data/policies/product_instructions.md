# VaaniDesk Product Instructions & Safety Hub

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## How to Use This Document

Product-specific manuals ship in-box and appear under **My Orders → Downloads**. This hub covers cross-category setup, safety, and troubleshooting patterns for VaaniDesk top categories.

## Electronics — First Power-On

1. Inspect for transit damage before powering on
2. Use only supplied charger or certified PD adapter (check wattage on product page)
3. Complete **8-hour charge cycle** for lithium devices before heavy use
4. Register serial on brand site within 7 days for full warranty

### Common Troubleshooting

- **No power:** Hold power 15 seconds; try alternate outlet
- **Overheating first boot:** Allow 30-minute index update on smart TVs
- **Pairing failure:** Reset Bluetooth accessory per manual section 4.2

## Appliances — Installation Requirements

Large appliances require **brand installation** within 48 hours of delivery for warranty validity. VaaniDesk schedules installation slot at checkout in serviceable cities.

- Washing machines: level surface, inlet pressure 0.03–1 MPa
- Refrigerators: stand upright **4 hours** before first plug-in if transported horizontal
- AC units: only licensed technician may handle refrigerant lines

## Furniture Assembly

VaaniDesk flat-pack furniture includes hex keys and labeled panels. Tips:

- Assemble on carpet or blanket to avoid chip
- Do not fully tighten screws until structure is square
- Wall anchoring kits recommended for units > 90 cm height in homes with children

Video guides: scan QR on instruction sheet or open app **Assembly → SKU search**.

## Consumables & Refills

Printer ink, water filters, robot vacuum bags: subscribe via **Subscribe & Save** for 10% discount. Match model number exactly—cross-compat list on product page.

## Safety Recalls

Active recalls listed at **vaanidesk.in/recalls**. Registered customers receive SMS/email. Stop use immediately; free remedy ships automatically when stock available.

## Disposal & E-Waste

Do not discard electronics in municipal waste. Request **E-Waste Pickup** in app (free once per year per account) or drop at authorized recycler map pin.
