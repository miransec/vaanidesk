# VaaniDesk Product Instructions — Extended Category Guides

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Companion to:** product_instructions.md

## Smart Home Devices

### Initial Setup Checklist

1. Download **VaaniDesk Home** or manufacturer app listed on box
2. Connect phone to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi during pairing (5 GHz often fails for IoT)
3. Firmware update may take 10–20 minutes—do not power cycle mid-update
4. Assign room names for voice assistant linking

### Troubleshooting Matrix

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| Device offline | Router channel change | Reboot router, re-add device |
| False motion alerts | PIR facing window/heat source | Reposition sensor |
| Hub buzzing | Overloaded USB | Use supplied power adapter only |

## Kitchen Appliances

### Microwave & OTG

- Remove all packaging films before first use
- Run **empty heat cycle** 2 minutes if odor from manufacturing oils
- Metal racks only as per manual—no foil unless combi mode allows

### Air Fryers

- Preheat 3 minutes for crisp results
- Do not overcrowd basket; single layer for fries
- Clean basket after each use to prevent smoke

## Personal Care Electronics

Trimmers, epilators, electric toothbrushes: charge fully before first use. IPX ratings on product page—do not submerge beyond stated rating.

Replace brush heads / blades per manufacturer interval (typically 3 months).

## Computing Peripherals

Mechanical keyboards: remove switch puller from accessory bag before attempting keycap removal.

Monitors: use DisplayPort or HDMI as rated; avoid cheap uncertified cables for > 144 Hz.

Docking stations: verify USB-C PD wattage meets laptop requirement (check product page compatibility matrix).

## Children's Products

CPSIA / BIS marked toys only as labeled. Remove plastic tie wraps before handing to child. Battery compartments require screwdriver—check screws present after battery change.

## Seasonal Products

**Monsoon care:** Store electronics with silica gel; run dehumidifier in storage rooms.

**Summer:** Avoid leaving devices in parked vehicles > 50°C ambient.
