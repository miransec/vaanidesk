# VaaniDesk Refund Timeline Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## Purpose

This policy consolidates **refund initiation**, **processing**, and **bank posting** timelines across cancellation, return, damage claim, and involuntary order termination scenarios. Timelines begin after VaaniDesk marks refund as **Initiated** in your order timeline unless stated otherwise.

## Refund Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending QC | Return received; inspection not complete |
| Refund Approved | Amount confirmed; awaiting payment rail |
| Initiated | File sent to payment aggregator / bank |
| Posted | Bank confirms credit (may differ from VaaniDesk status) |
| Failed | Invalid instrument; customer action required |

## Timelines by Scenario

### Pre-Shipment Cancellation

| Payment type | Initiation | Customer sees credit |
|--------------|------------|---------------------|
| UPI | Same day | 1–3 business days |
| Credit / debit card | Same day | 3–5 business days |
| Net banking | 1 business day | 3–5 business days |
| VaaniDesk Wallet | Instant | Instant |
| EMI | 2 business days | 7–14 business days (lender dependent) |

### Post-Return QC Approval

| Payment type | Initiation after QC | Customer sees credit |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| UPI | 1 business day | 3–5 business days |
| Cards | 1 business day | 5–7 business days |
| COD orders | 2 business days | Bank transfer 5–7 business days |
| Wallet (original) | Same day | Instant |
| Gift card split | Partial to wallet per ledger rules | 1–3 business days |

### Damage / DOA Immediate Refund

Approved total-loss damage claims initiate within **24 hours** regardless of payment method except EMI (lender schedule applies).

## Partial Refunds

Price adjustments, missing accessory credits, or goodwill partials post as separate ledger entries with reference to original order line.

## Failed Refund Handling

If status shows **Failed**:

1. Verify bank account / UPI VPA in profile
2. Update instrument within **7 days** via secure link
3. Unclaimed refunds escheat to customer wallet after 30 days with email notice

## Interest & Compensation

VaaniDesk does not pay interest on delayed refunds. If delay exceeds **2× published SLA**, Plus members may request **₹100 wallet credit** (once per order, automated eligibility check).

## Reconciliation Tips

Match **RRN / ARN / UTR** from refund email to bank statement. Credit may appear as separate line from original debit descriptor.
