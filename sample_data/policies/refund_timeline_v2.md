# VaaniDesk Refund Timeline Policy (v2)

**Effective date:** 15 April 2026

## Instant Wallet Credit Option

Customers may opt **Instant Wallet Credit** at return approval for full refund amount minus optional ₹50 fast-lane fee waived for Plus members. Wallet credits withdraw to bank per wallet policy.

## Real-Time UPI Refund Rail

Eligible UPI refunds (original VPA active, amount < ₹50,000) now target **T+0 posting** within business hours 9:00–21:00 IST when marked Initiated.

## EMI Clarification

No-cost EMI cancellations: lender must confirm subvention reversal within **10 business days**; VaaniDesk publishes lender reference number in order notes.

## Holiday Blackout

Bank holidays in Maharashtra and Karnataka may add +1 business day to card refunds initiated on holiday eve—calendar published annually at **vaanidesk.in/refund-calendar**.
