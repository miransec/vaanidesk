# VaaniDesk Return Procedure (v2)

**Effective date:** 1 June 2026

## Extended Return Window Pilot

VaaniDesk Plus members receive **15-day return window** on fashion and home décor categories (badge: **Plus Extended Returns**).

## QR Drop-off at Partner Stores

Show in-app **Return QR** at partner retail counters—no printed label required. Staff scan and provide thermal receipt. Available in 120 cities.

## QC Photo Transparency

Customers receive **QC photo set** when inspection completes, showing condition notes. Dispute QC rejection within **48 hours** with counter-evidence.

## Consolidated Returns

Multiple open RMAs from same address may **merge pickup** into single courier visit when initiated within same calendar day.

## Environmental Handling

Electronics returns must exclude swollen batteries from mail—request **hazard pickup** flag in return flow.

## Hindi / Marathi

Plus members: **15-day** fashion/home returns.  
QR drop-off 120 cities.  
QC photos shared; 48-hour dispute window.
