# VaaniDesk Warranty Terms

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## General Framework

VaaniDesk sells products covered by **manufacturer warranty** unless the product page states **VaaniDesk Extended Care**. Warranty periods start from **delivery date** (or installation date for white-goods with mandatory installation).

## Standard Manufacturer Warranty

| Category | Typical warranty | Service type |
|----------|------------------|--------------|
| Mobiles & tablets | 1 year | Brand ASC |
| Laptops | 1–3 years | Brand ASC |
| Large appliances | 1–2 years | Brand technician visit |
| Accessories (cables, cases) | 6 months | Replace via VaaniDesk if brand N/A |
| Furniture (VaaniDesk brand) | 12 months structural | VaaniDesk direct |

Always retain invoice PDF from order history—required for all warranty claims.

## VaaniDesk Extended Care (Optional Purchase)

Purchased at checkout or within 15 days of delivery:

- Extends coverage up to **+2 years** on eligible electronics
- Includes **1 accidental damage** incident per term (₹999 deductible)
- Transferable once if product gifted (KYC update required)

Extended Care is **non-refundable** after 15-day cooling period.

## What Warranty Covers

- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- DOA failures after damaged-products window closes (rare—escalate to warranty team)
- Battery capacity below 80% within year 1 for phones (brand-specific)

## What Warranty Does Not Cover

- Physical abuse, liquid damage (unless Accidental Damage rider active)
- Unauthorized repairs or flashed/rooted software modifications
- Consumables: filters, ink, drum units, batteries after stated cycle count
- Commercial use unless SKU marked business-grade

## Claim Process

1. **My Orders → Warranty → Start Claim**
2. Enter serial number and issue description
3. Upload diagnostic report if requested (especially laptops)
4. Receive ASC appointment or shipping label within 3 business days

Repair SLA target: **15 business days** from ASC receipt (parts availability may extend).

## Out-of-Warranty Support

VaaniDesk offers paid repair quotes via partner network. Declining repair returns unit at shipping cost to customer.
